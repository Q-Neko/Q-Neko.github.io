# Q-Neko Kickoff Workshop

The Q-Neko consortium gathered in Tokyo for the project kickoff workshop, marking the official start of joint work between European and Japanese partners.

## Highlights

- Presentation of the five pillars of the project
- Working group formation for HPC+AI+QC integration
- Bilateral discussions on the researcher exchange programme
